🧪 Bangla Phonetic Pronunciation — Chrome Test Page

🎯 How to test:

  1. Hold down the Ctrl key on your keyboard.
  2. Select any English word or phrase below with your mouse.
  3. Release the mouse button — you will see the Bengali phonetic pronunciation appear right below your selection!
  4. Click anywhere else on the page or press Esc to dismiss the pronunciation.
  5. Note: Normal selection (without holding Ctrl) will NOT trigger any pronunciation popup.
